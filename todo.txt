offer actions timing
{
The 1-minute delay is caused by synchronous email sending blocking the thread.
In Spring, the @Async annotation only works when a method is called from outside the bean (via the Spring proxy). In your EmailService.java, the method sendNotificationEmail() is missing the @Async annotation, and it calls sendHtmlEmail(). Because this is a self-invocation (internal call within the same class), the @Async on sendHtmlEmail is bypassed, and the app waits for the slow SMTP server to respond before finishing the offer update.
Steps
Add @Async directly to sendNotificationEmail in EmailService.java.
Add @Async to all other specialized email methods in EmailService.java that are called from external services (sendVerificationEmail, sendPasswordResetEmail, sendCongratulationsEmail, sendTwoFactorAuthEmail, sendAdminInviteEmail).
(Optional) In DocumentService.java, ensure PdfRendererBuilder and template compilation aren't excessively blocking, but fixing the email execution will likely solve 95% of the delay.
Spring requires #setThreadNamePrefix on your async task executor config to easily spot async threads in the logs and confirm this is working.
}
